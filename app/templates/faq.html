Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about volunteering and donations.

How do I sign up to volunteer?

Go to the submissions form, select Volunteer, complete your details, and submit your hours.

How does volunteer time generate funding?

Volunteer hours are tracked and converted into value using the project hourly rate configured by the admin.

Can I submit volunteer hours after completing them?

Yes. Enter the date you completed the work and provide a clear description in the form.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Tax treatment depends on your local laws and organization status, so verify with your tax advisor.

How can I see the impact of my contribution?

Admins can review totals, hours, and project summaries from the dashboard to track impact over time.

What types of volunteer work qualify?

Any approved project-related service qualifies, including setup, logistics, cleanup, and support roles.

Can I volunteer if I am under 18?

It depends on project rules. Some projects may require guardian consent or minimum age requirements.

How long will the recovery project be active?

Project timelines vary. Check the selected project details page for current status and availability.

Still have questions?

Contact your project coordinator or admin team for additional support.
