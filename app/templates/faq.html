Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about volunteering and donations.

How do I sign up to volunteer?

Go to the submissions form, select Volunteer, complete your details, and submit your hours.

How does volunteer time generate funding?

Volunteer hours are tracked and converted into value using the disaster hourly rate configured by the admin.

Can I submit volunteer hours after completing them?

Yes. Enter the date you completed the work and provide a clear description in the form.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Tax treatment depends on your local laws and organization status, so verify with your tax advisor.

How can I see the impact of my contribution?

Admins can review totals, hours, and disaster summaries from the dashboard to track impact over time.

What types of volunteer work qualify?

Any approved disaster-related service qualifies, including setup, logistics, cleanup, and support roles.

Can I volunteer if I am under 18?

It depends on disaster rules. Some disasters may require guardian consent or minimum age requirements.

How long will the recovery disaster be active?

Disaster timelines vary. Check the selected disaster details page for current status and availability.

Still have questions?

Contact your disaster coordinator or admin team for additional support.
