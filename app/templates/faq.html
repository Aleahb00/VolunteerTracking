Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about volunteering and donations.

Is there a deadline to submit my form?

Please submit your form as soon as possible after completing your volunteer work to ensure accurate tracking.

How does volunteer time generate funding?

Volunteer hours are tracked and reported, which can help secure funding, grants, or support by showing community involvement and the impact of the work completed.

How do I document multiple days of work?

For multiple days of work, submit a separate form for each day.

Why are you asking for my information?

We collect basic information to verify volunteer hours and ensure accurate record-keeping.

Who do I contact if I have questions or issues with the form?

A: If you have any questions, please reach out using the contact information provided on this page.

What types of volunteer work qualify?

Any approved project-related service qualifies, including setup, logistics, cleanup, and support roles.

Do I fill out the form before or after I volunteer?

AFTER completing your volunteer work, since the form is used to document the work performed.

Can someone submit a form on my behalf?

No, each volunteer should submit their own form to ensure accuracy.

Still have questions?

Contact us at dr@erassist.com or volunteer@erassist.com
